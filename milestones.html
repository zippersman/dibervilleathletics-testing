HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

Season milestones, not career records

Milestones are calculated from the current values in each team's published roster sheet. Thresholds are site-defined recognition levels, not official school records or awards. A player may appear more than once if they reach multiple milestones. These totals do not combine seasons, and player identity is not assumed across teams or years.

Only statistics actually present in a source sheet are evaluated. Missing values are not treated as zero and are never estimated.

Data source: the currently published D’Iberville Athletics team statistics sheets. Last checked time and source availability are shown above.
