GET THE APP

Add it to your home screen

Put D’Iberville Athletics on your phone like a regular app: one tap to open, full screen, no browser bars. It’s free and takes about 15 seconds.

iPhone & iPad

Use Safari

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave the name as “DHS Stats” and tap Add.

Don’t see it? Tap “More” or scroll the share sheet. On iPhones with newer iOS versions, other browsers like Chrome can do this too, but Safari always works.

Android

Use Chrome

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install or Add to confirm.

Using Samsung Internet or Firefox? Open the menu and look for “Add page to” → “Home screen” or “Install.”

Done! Look for the gold DHS icon on your home screen and open it from there.
